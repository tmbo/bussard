Add a NEW device on the PHYSICAL bus, the flow of `bussard adopt` (issue #749): press the button, identify, assign the address, fetch the product data, write the device file. Three steps. (1) Call with optional `address` (else the next free one), `order_number` and `product` (a path to the device's .knxprod, imported into the product store first, as `bussard adopt --product`; then no download is asked): answers at once with `job` and `question` (press the programming button): relay it word for word, then poll knx_job_status. The job waits up to 30 s for the device. (2) knx_job_status reports step `identified`: serial, mask, order number, `product_data` (in the store, or what would be downloaded), `sentences`, `plan_digest` and `question`. Nothing is written yet: show the sentences and ask the question. (3) ONLY after the human's explicit yes in this conversation, call again with `job`, `plan_digest` and `yes: true`, plus `download: true` when `download_needed` is true and the human agreed to the download too. It re-reads the device and refuses when it changed, writes the address, reads the device back at the new address and writes the device file and its lock entry: the result has `step: done`, `assigned`, `verified` (the read-back matches the identified serial and mask), `summary`, `objects` to wire and `next_step`. Refuses an address another device answers at. A device the keyring lists is adopted where ETS put it over A_SecureData; securing a factory-fresh device stays with ETS. Only available with --allow-programming.
