Replace a DEAD device with the same product on the PHYSICAL bus, the flow of `bussard replace` (issue #751): backup, press the button, identify, then the address, the application, the project's parameters and links and the record, with one yes. Three calls. (1) Call with `address` (the dead device's): it snapshots the project (`backup`: the device file and its bussard.lock record, kept in the history) and answers at once with `job` and `question` (press the programming button on the replacement): tell the human what `backup` kept, relay the question word for word, then poll knx_job_status. The job first checks that the address no longer answers (a device that still answers is refused), then waits up to 30 s for the replacement. (2) knx_job_status reports step `identified`: serial, mask, order number, the resident application, `identity` against bussard.lock, `application` and `product_data` (the pinned archive, or what would be downloaded), `sentences`, `plan_digest` and `question`. Another product (order number or mask) is refused. Nothing is written yet: show the sentences and ask the question. (3) ONLY after the human's explicit yes in this conversation, call again with `job`, `plan_digest` and `yes: true`, plus `download: true` when `download_needed` is true and the human agreed to the download too. It re-reads the device and refuses when it changed, writes the address and reads it back, then plans and writes the device as knx_plan_device and knx_apply_device do (the pinned application loaded when the replacement does not run it, the backups, the independent read-back) as the same job, and records `replaced` in the device file and the new serial in bussard.lock. The result has `step: done`, `ok`, `verified`, `assigned`, `flashed`, `write` (the apply result), `recorded`, `summary` and `next_step`; when the address took but the write did not verify, `ok: false` with `reason` and `recovery`. A device the project records as secured is refused: securing a factory-fresh device stays with ETS. Only available with --allow-programming.
