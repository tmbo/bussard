The certificate enrolment session started by knx_enroll_certificates: `session` (`running`, `ended` or `none`), `end` (`phone_done`, `stopped`, `connect_timeout`, `idle_timeout`, `max_duration`, `expired` or `error`), `phones`, `refused_phones` and `warning` (another phone tried to join: tell the human first), `certificates` and `stored` (counts), and `results` once the session ended or with `details: true` (only when the human asks; the pairing page lists them), one entry per certificate in arrival order with `serial`, `address` and `name` of the matched device, `status` (`stored`: saved on that device; `unassigned`: saved under its serial until a device reports it; `already_stored`; `conflict`: the store holds another key for the serial, nothing written; `invalid`; `error`) and `message`. Never a certificate's code or a key. `tally` counts the statuses (`stored`, `waiting`, `already_known`, `conflicts`, `invalid`, `errors`); `summary` says it in one line, and once the session has ended `next_steps` lists the CLI commands that apply (`command`, `why`). `wait_seconds` (at most 120) waits for the next certificate or the end first; `until_end: true` waits for the end of the session instead, as long as the chat app allows (`wait_seconds` caps it); `stop: true` ends the session. Reads nothing from the bus.
