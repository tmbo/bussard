This server runs without --allow-programming, so edits stay in the files. Restarted with that flag, knx_plan_device and knx_apply_device push a device's links and parameter values, knx_add_device adds a new device, knx_commission_line commissions a line, knx_replace_device replaces a dead one, knx_restore_tables writes a backup's link tables back and knx_restart_device restarts a device; without it, they are `bussard plan <ia>`, `bussard apply <ia>`, `bussard adopt`, `bussard commission --line <line>`, `bussard replace <ia>`, `bussard restore <backup> <ia>` and `bussard restart <ia>` at the CLI.
