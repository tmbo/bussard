Observe with knx_recent_telegrams and knx_wait_for_telegram (the latter enables 'press the button now' debugging). Passive mode: nothing is transmitted, so knx_read_group, knx_describe_device, knx_backup_device, knx_reconstruct_device and knx_find_devices are not available, nor knx_show_device's live read. knx_bus_status says why the bus is not connected (the last transport error, the attempts, the next retry).
